# Development milestones (targets, not guarantees)

**November 2026–February 2027:** Engine project, small test map, third-person movement, basic weapon, two-client authoritative test.

**February–May 2027:** Zombie AI, replicated damage and inventory, first two-player session.

**May–November 2027:** One detailed district, 30–60 minute vertical slice, story integration, private 4-player co-op target.

**November 2027–May 2028:** Dedicated-server prototype, persistent player/world state, reconnect testing, 4–8 player test.

**May–November 2028:** Alpha polish, optimization, stability and scaling tests. Larger public populations remain a later, performance-gated goal.

Scope is reviewed after every milestone; working gameplay takes precedence over feature count.
